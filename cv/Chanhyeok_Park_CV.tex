\section{Research Profile}
I am Chanhyeok Park, a graduate student in the integrated M.S.--Ph.D. program at KAIST AI Future Studies, where I work under the guidance of \link{https://fs.kaist.ac.kr/ko/?c=184&s=&cidx1=41&gp=1&gbn=viewok&ix=1545}{Prof. Woojung Jon}. I earned my bachelor's degree from the KAIST School of Computing. Alongside my academic work, I am Co-Founder and CTO of \textbf{IP Intelligence}.

\smallskip
My research centers on \textbf{AI safety}, which I approach through two complementary directions.
\par\vspace{5pt}
\noindent\begin{minipage}[t]{0.477\linewidth}
\raggedright\hyphenpenalty=10000\relax
In \textbf{legal research}, I study AI governance and regulation, including frameworks such as the EU AI Act. I examine how risk-based regulation, legal accountability, and institutional oversight should be designed to prevent harm and protect fundamental rights throughout AI development and deployment.
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.477\linewidth}
\raggedright\hyphenpenalty=10000\relax
In \textbf{technical research}, I explore how large language models (LLMs) and agentic AI can be trained, evaluated, and deployed safely. My interests include safety alignment, adversarial testing, and safeguards against harmful outputs and unintended agent actions.
\end{minipage}\par
